The plates

Every lens in detail.

Plate I

Tongues

Language

The language each profile reaches for first.

How it is read

The language tag on each post decides it. When a post has no tag, the writing script (Devanagari, Arabic, Cyrillic and so on) gives it away. Mixed speech such as Hinglish keeps its own line.

Plate II

Territories

Region

Where the crowd says it lives, folded into world regions.

How it is read

City and country names in the location field are matched to a world region. Exact places are never shown, and a blank or joke location counts as not stated.

Plate III

Callings

Interest

What people do, or love doing, in their own words.

How it is read

Keyword rules read each bio for work and hobby themes. A bio can carry several, so these shares add up past 100%. In the constellation each dot sits in its first match.

Plate IV

Seasons

Faint signal

A hint of life stage, from words like “student” or “retired”.

How it is read

Most bios give no hint at all. This is a group-level estimate from self-described words and says nothing about any one person.

Plate V

Rhythm

Posting hours · UTC

When the crowd posts, hour by hour. The rows follow the lens you picked above.

0006121823
How it is read

Each wedge adds up posts in that UTC hour; longer means busier. A region that posts late at night in UTC is usually awake in the evening where it lives. Hours are counted for every post by the profiles in view.

Plate VI

Reach

Followers

Mostly small voices, and a few loud ones.

How it is read

Public follower counts, grouped into five tiers. Bubble area follows the number of profiles in each tier.

Plate VII

Tenure

Account age

When their accounts were opened.

How it is read

Public join dates, grouped into two-year cohorts. A tall right-hand column means a young audience on that platform.

Plate VIII

Vocabulary

Bio words

The words people use to introduce themselves. Tap one and its owners light up in the constellation above.

How it is read

Only words used by at least 5 profiles appear, and common filler words are dropped. Larger means more profiles. Italic words are hashtags. The bios themselves are never shown.

How the atlas is drawn

Four steps, and a few things it will never do.

1

Read

Public signals only: the bio, the stated place, the language tag, posting hours, follower count and join date.

2

Match

Plain keyword rules and script detection. No model is trained and nothing leaves your browser.

3

Count

One light is one anonymous ID. Brand and official accounts are set aside, not counted as audience.

4

Gate

Any group under 5 folds into Other. Any word used by fewer than 5 profiles never appears.
