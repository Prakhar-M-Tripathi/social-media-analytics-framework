How this works, and its limits

  • Input: the shared data/posts.csv. If it lacks the optional columns language, user_bio and user_location, they are joined by post_id from Section A’s sidecar files. You can also load your own CSV.
  • Method: transparent keyword rules and script detection run in your browser. No model is trained, no data leaves the page, and nothing is sent to a server.
  • Unit: one profile is one anonymised user_id. The same person on two platforms counts twice. Brand or official accounts are excluded from audience figures.
  • Estimates, not facts: bios and locations are self-reported, short and sometimes jokes. Rules miss many profiles (see the “not available” counts) and can mislabel others. Life-stage cues are the weakest signal here.
  • Not inferred: gender, ethnicity, religion, politics, health or exact identity.
