How it works, and its limits

  • Data: the shared data/posts.csv when it has profile columns; otherwise Section C’s own synthetic sample/audience_sample.csv. You can also load your own CSV with user_id and the optional language, user_bio, user_location columns.
  • Method: transparent keyword rules and writing-script detection, run in your browser. No model is trained, nothing is uploaded, and no server is involved.
  • Unit: one dot is one anonymised user_id. The same person on two platforms counts twice. Brand or official accounts are excluded from the audience.
  • Estimates, not facts: bios and locations are self-reported, short and sometimes jokes. Rules miss many profiles (see the “not available” counts) and can mislabel others. Life-stage cues are the weakest signal here.
  • Not inferred: gender, ethnicity, religion, politics, health or identity.
