The whisperers

Four ways to matter, and they rarely agree.

Plate I

Four ways to matter

Rank

Each line is one voice. Watch it climb in one column and fall in the next.

How it is read

Weight is PageRank on who answers whom. Most answered counts incoming replies, quotes, reposts and shares. Bridges is betweenness: how often a voice sits on the shortest route between two others. Sparks counts everything that grew from posts they started.

Plate II

The cast

Roles

Everyone plays a part. Click a dot to find it on the vine.

How it is read

Bridges are the top 8% by betweenness and touch at least two circles. Hubs are the top 10% by PageRank. Sparks are the top 8% by downstream reach. Amplifiers pass things on far more than they are answered. Listeners are never answered.

The plates

The vine, cut open and labelled.

Plate III

Anatomy of a vine

Cascades

One post, and everything that grew from it. Leaves mark where the word stopped.

How it is read

Each branch is a reply, quote, repost or share of the post before it. Dots are coloured by mood: green positive, amber neutral, rose negative. Virality is the average number of hops between any two posts in the tree: a broadcast scores low, a true person-to-person spread scores high.

Plate IV

The relay

Spread over time

Who passed the baton to whom. The bright line joins each group’s first post on the trend.

How it is read

Rows are ordered by who picked the trend up first. In Volume view brighter means more posts that day. In Mood view green is positive and rose is negative. The strip underneath shows the trend’s overall mood drifting day by day.

Plate V

Neighbourhoods

Circles

Circles the algorithm found by itself, and the few ropes between them.

How it is read

Louvain community detection groups voices that talk to each other more than chance would predict. Each circle is named after its usual platform and calling. Ribbons show exchanges between circles; chatter inside a circle is left out so the rare crossings can be seen.

Plate VI

Who answers whom

Mixing

Read across: of everything a group says, how much goes to each other group.

How it is read

The diagonal is a group talking to itself. A bright diagonal means an echo chamber. The tag above compares the share of exchanges inside groups with what random mixing would give.

Plate VII

Border crossings

Cross-platform

Word leaving one platform for another. Arcs above run left to right, arcs below run back.

How it is read

A crossing is a post shared from one platform to another. Thicker arcs carry more of them. Direction follows the word: from the platform it was first said on to the one that picked it up.

Plate VIII

Mood is catching

Sentiment spread

Row is the post being answered. Column is the answer it got.

How it is read

Each cell is the share of answers to that kind of post. The multiplier compares it with how common that mood is overall, so 1.8× means nearly twice as likely as usual. Reposts are left out because they copy the original.

Plate IX

The waiting room

Reaction time

How long a post waits before someone reacts.

How it is read

Time between a post and each reaction to it, in widening buckets.

Plate X

The long tail

Degree

Many voices heard by a few. A few voices heard by many.

How it is read

Both axes are logarithmic. A straight, falling line is the mark of a heavy-tailed network, and the tag above shows its estimated slope.

Plate XI

The breaking point

Robustness

Remove voices one by one. How much of the vine still holds together?

How it is read

The height of each line is the share of the largest connected group that survives. Targeted cuts usually fall faster than random ones. Try it live with the Cut the wires tool above.

How the vine is grown

Four steps, and a few things it refuses to do.

1

Thread

Every reply, quote, repost and share becomes a line from one anonymous voice to another.

2

Weigh

PageRank, betweenness and k-core score each voice, right here in your browser.

3

Group

Louvain finds the circles. Nobody tells it what to look for.

4

Trace

Cascades rebuild each trend hop by hop, and every group under 5 people is folded away.
