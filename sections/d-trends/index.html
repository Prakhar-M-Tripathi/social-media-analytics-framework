Section D: Trend and Topic Detection

Finds the keywords, hashtags and topics people are talking about, ranks which ones are rising fastest, and predicts which are likely to keep rising.

1. Rising topics ranked

Posts are grouped into topics by the words they share. Each topic gets a trend score that compares its recent activity with its earlier activity. A score above 1 means it is growing.

Number of posts per topic in each time step. Dotted lines show the forecast for the next three steps.

6. Topic details

Pick a topic to see its keywords, platforms and example posts.

How it works

  1. Clean: each post is lower cased, links and stop words (English, Hindi, Hinglish) are removed. Hindi script is kept.
  2. Find: words, two word phrases and hashtags are counted per time step.
  3. Group into topics: frequent terms that appear together in the same posts are joined into one topic (co‑occurrence clustering). Each post goes to the topic it matches best.
  4. Rank: trend score = posts per step in the recent window (last 25% of time) ÷ posts per step before it, with smoothing so tiny counts do not explode. A burst score shows how unusual the latest step is compared with the topic's normal level.
  5. Predict: a straight line is fitted to the last 7 steps of each topic and extended 3 steps ahead. A topic is marked Likely to rise when the line slopes clearly upward and the trend score is above 1.

Input: data/posts.csv using the shared columns (post_id, platform, user_id, timestamp, text, parent_id). Until that file exists the page uses Section A's demo file. Everything runs in your browser; no data is sent anywhere. Results are estimates from word patterns, not certainties.
